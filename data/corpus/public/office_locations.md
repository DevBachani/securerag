# Office Locations

**Owner:** Facilities Team · **Audience:** All employees

Acme Corp has approximately **400 employees** across three offices in India. Standard
office hours are **09:30 to 18:30**, Monday to Friday.

## Bengaluru (Headquarters)
- Location: Koramangala, Bengaluru
- Capacity: **220 seats**
- Houses Engineering, Leadership and central functions.

## Pune
- Location: Baner, Pune
- Capacity: **90 seats**
- Primarily Sales and Customer Success.

## Hyderabad
- Location: Gachibowli, Hyderabad
- Capacity: **70 seats**
- Primarily Finance and Support.

## Access
Office entry uses employee access cards. Visitors must be pre-registered at reception at
least one day in advance. Hot-desking is available in Pune and Hyderabad.
