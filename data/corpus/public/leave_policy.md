# Leave Policy

**Owner:** People Team · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Audience:** All employees

## Earned leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to **24 earned leaves per year**, accrued at
**2 days per month**. Up to **30 earned leaves** may be carried forward to the next
financial year. Any balance above **15 days** is encashed at the end of the financial
year at basic pay.

## Casual and sick leave
Employees receive **7 casual leaves** and **12 sick leaves** per year. Casual leave
cannot be carried forward. Sick leave beyond 2 consecutive days requires a medical
certificate.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is **26 weeks** as per Indian law. Paternity leave is **10 working days**,
to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Applying for leave
Planned leave must be requested at least **7 days** in advance through the HR portal and
approved by the reporting manager. Leave during probation is unpaid unless approved as an
exception.
