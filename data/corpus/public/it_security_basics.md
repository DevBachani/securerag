# IT Security Basics

**Owner:** Security Team · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Audience:** All employees

## Passwords and access
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and are rotated every 90 days.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is mandatory for all company accounts.
- Laptops auto-lock after **5 minutes** of inactivity.

## Data classification
Acme uses **four data classification levels**: Public, Internal, Confidential and
Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data may not be copied to personal devices or
cloud storage.

## Devices and network
- **VPN** is required to reach internal applications from outside the office.
- **USB mass storage** is disabled on all company laptops.
- Software may only be installed from the approved software catalogue.

## Reporting incidents
Suspected phishing or security incidents must be reported to **secops@acme.example**
immediately. The average target response time for a reported incident is **1 hour**.
