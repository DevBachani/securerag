# Travel & Expense Policy (v1.0 - SUPERSEDED)

**Owner:** Finance Team · **Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** 1 July 2025 · **Audience:** All employees

> This is the OLD travel policy (v1.0). It has been replaced by v2.0. The figures
> below are out of date and must not be used.

## Per diem
- Domestic travel: **INR 2,000 per day** for meals and incidentals.
- International travel: **USD 60 per day** for meals and incidentals.

## Flights
- Flights under **5 hours**: economy class only.
- Tickets must be booked at least **7 days** in advance to qualify for reimbursement.

## Hotels
- Metro cities: up to **INR 5,000 per night**.
- Non-metro cities: up to **INR 3,500 per night**.

## Reimbursement
Claims must be submitted with receipts within **45 days** of trip completion.
