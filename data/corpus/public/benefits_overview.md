# Benefits Overview

**Owner:** People Team · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Audience:** All employees

## Health and insurance
- Group health insurance: **₹5,00,000** family floater (employee, spouse, 2 children, 2 parents).
- Term life insurance: **3x annual CTC**.
- Annual preventive health check-up, fully paid.

## Allowances
- Wellness allowance: **₹15,000 per year** (gym, therapy, fitness).
- Learning & development budget: **₹20,000 per employee per year**.
- Meal card: **₹2,200 per month**, tax-advantaged.

## Equity
Employees become eligible for **ESOPs after 1 year** of service. Grants vest over 4 years
with a 1-year cliff.

## Other
- Internet reimbursement of ₹1,000 per month for remote work days.
- Relocation support for transfers between Acme offices.
