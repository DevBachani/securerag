# Onboarding Guide

**Owner:** People Team · **Audience:** All new employees

Welcome to Acme Corp. This guide covers your first weeks.

## Before day one
HR shares your offer paperwork and a pre-joining checklist. Your laptop is provisioned and
delivered within **2 working days** of your start date.

## Day one
- Collect your access card from reception.
- Complete identity and bank verification with HR.
- Meet your **onboarding buddy**, who supports you through your first month.

## Probation
All new hires serve a **6-month probation period**. Confirmation depends on a manager review
at the end of probation.

## First week training
Mandatory sessions cover security awareness, the code of conduct, and your team's tools. The
**IT helpdesk** is reachable at extension **1800** for any setup issues.

## First 90 days
You and your manager agree on 30/60/90-day goals during your first week. A formal check-in
happens at the end of 90 days.
