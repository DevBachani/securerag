# Code of Conduct

**Owner:** People Team · **Effective:** 1 April 2026 · **Audience:** All employees

## Our principles
Acme Corp operates on integrity, respect and accountability. Harassment or discrimination
of any kind results in **zero-tolerance** disciplinary action, up to termination.

## Conflict of interest
Employees must disclose any outside financial interest in a customer, vendor or competitor.
Gifts from business partners worth more than **₹25,000** must be declined or reported to the
Ethics Committee.

## Anti-bribery
No employee may offer or accept bribes or kickbacks. Facilitation payments are prohibited
under Acme's anti-corruption standard.

## Whistleblower channel
Concerns can be raised confidentially at **ethics@acme.example**. Retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is itself a serious violation.

## Workplace standards
Dress code is **business casual**. Alcohol and controlled substances are prohibited on
company premises. Violations are reviewed by the People Team under the disciplinary process.
