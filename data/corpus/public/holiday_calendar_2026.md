# Holiday Calendar 2026

**Owner:** People Team · **Audience:** All employees

Acme Corp observes **12 public holidays** in the 2026 calendar year, plus **3 optional
(floating) holidays** that each employee may choose from a published list.

## Fixed public holidays 2026
| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 26 January 2026 | Republic Day |
| 25 March 2026 | Holi |
| 3 April 2026 | Good Friday |
| 1 May 2026 | Maharashtra/Labour Day |
| 15 August 2026 | Independence Day |
| 2 October 2026 | Gandhi Jayanti |
| 20 October 2026 | Dussehra |
| 8 November 2026 | Diwali |
| 9 November 2026 | Govardhan Puja |
| 25 November 2026 | Guru Nanak Jayanti |
| 25 December 2026 | Christmas |
| 31 December 2026 | Year-End Day |

## Optional holidays
Each employee may take **3 optional holidays** from the floating list (e.g. Eid, Onam,
Pongal, Raksha Bandhan). Optional holidays must be booked at least 3 days in advance.
