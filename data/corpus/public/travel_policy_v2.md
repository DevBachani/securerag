# Travel & Expense Policy (v2.0)

**Owner:** Finance Team · **Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** 1 July 2026 · **Audience:** All employees

This version (v2.0) replaces the earlier travel policy. Where figures differ, **v2.0 is
authoritative**.

## Per diem
- Domestic travel: **₹2,500 per day** for meals and incidentals.
- International travel: **USD 75 per day** for meals and incidentals.

## Flights
- Flights under **6 hours**: economy class only.
- Flights of 6 hours or more: premium economy permitted with manager approval.
- Tickets must be booked at least **14 days** in advance to qualify for reimbursement.

## Hotels
- Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad): up to **₹6,000 per night**.
- Non-metro cities: up to **₹4,000 per night**.

## Reimbursement
Claims must be submitted with receipts within **30 days** of trip completion. Claims
submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval. Personal expenses and alcohol
are not reimbursable.
