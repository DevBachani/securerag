# Board Meeting Notes — Q2 FY27

**Owner:** CEO Office · **Sensitivity:** Restricted · **Audience:** Leadership only

Minutes of the Acme Corp board meeting for the second quarter of FY27.

## Financial highlights
- Q2 FY27 revenue: **₹62 crore**, up **18% year on year**.
- EBITDA margin: **14%**.
- Cash runway at current burn: **22 months**.

## Key decisions
1. Approved expansion of the **ESOP pool to 8%** of fully diluted equity.
2. Approved the FY27 hiring plan (net +80 headcount).
3. Authorised management to begin **exploratory M&A diligence** (see the M&A shortlist).

## Board composition
The board has **7 seats**: 3 founders, 2 investor seats and 2 independent directors. One
independent seat is open and being filled.

## Risks discussed
Customer concentration (top 3 customers are 38% of revenue) and cloud cost growth were
flagged as the two priority risks for FY27.
