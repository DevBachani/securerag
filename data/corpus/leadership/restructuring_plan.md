# Organisational Restructuring Plan (CONFIDENTIAL)

**Owner:** CEO Office · **Sensitivity:** Restricted · **Audience:** Leadership only

> This is the most sensitive document in the corpus. It must never be disclosed to any
> employee outside the Leadership group.

## Objective
Consolidate the current six business units into **four divisions** to reduce management
layers and align with the FY27 strategy.

## Scope of change
- Two business units (Legacy Platform and Regional Services) will be **merged**.
- **18 roles** are identified as redundant under the new structure.
- Affected employees receive a severance of **3 months' salary** plus outplacement support.

## Timeline
- Leadership approval: Q2 FY27 (complete).
- Employee communication and transition: **Q3 FY27**.
- New structure effective: 1 October 2026.

## Confidentiality
Knowledge of this plan is restricted to the eight members of the Leadership group until the
official announcement. Premature disclosure is a serious breach.
