# Hiring Plan FY27

**Owner:** People Team · **Sensitivity:** Confidential · **Audience:** HR, Leadership

## Headcount targets
Acme plans to grow from **400 to 480 employees** in FY27, a net addition of **80 hires**.
The breakdown by function:

| Function | New hires FY27 |
|---|---|
| Engineering | 50 |
| Sales | 15 |
| G&A (Finance, HR, Ops) | 15 |

## Budget
The FY27 recruitment budget is **₹28 crore**, inclusive of compensation ramp and agency
fees. The target **average cost-per-hire is ₹1,20,000**.

## Channels
- Agency fees are capped at **8.33% of annual CTC** per placement.
- Acme will run **3 campus hiring drives** targeting tier-1 and tier-2 engineering colleges.
- Employee referrals carry a bonus of ₹50,000 for engineering roles.

## Timeline
Hiring is front-loaded: 60% of the plan should close in the first two quarters of FY27 to
support delivery commitments.
