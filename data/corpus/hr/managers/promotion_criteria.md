# Promotion Criteria by Level

**Owner:** People Team · **Sensitivity:** Confidential · **Audience:** Managers, HR, Leadership

## Career levels
Acme uses seven engineering levels, **L1 through L7**. L1–L2 are early career, L3–L4 are
senior individual contributors, L5–L7 are staff/principal and leadership.

## Baseline requirement
Promotion to any level requires a performance rating of **4 or higher for two consecutive
cycles**, plus meeting the skill matrix for the target level.

## Level-specific gates
- **L2 → L3:** owns a feature end to end and mentors at least one junior.
- **L3 → L4:** demonstrated cross-team impact and **mentoring of 2 juniors**.
- **L4 → L5:** leads a technical initiative spanning multiple quarters.

## Budget and cycle
- The annual **promotion budget is capped at 12% of team cost**.
- Promotions are decided **once a year in April** during the review calibration.

Promotion decisions are confidential until communicated to the employee by their manager.
