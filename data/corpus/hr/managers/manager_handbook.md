# Manager Handbook

**Owner:** People Team · **Sensitivity:** Confidential · **Audience:** Managers, HR, Leadership

## Team structure
Target span of control is **6 to 8 direct reports** per manager. Teams larger than 10
should be split or add a team lead.

## Cadence
- **Weekly 1:1s** with every direct report are mandatory.
- Monthly team retrospectives are expected.

## Approval authority
- Managers approve leave requests for their reports up to **15 consecutive days**; longer
  requests escalate to HR.
- Managers approve expenses up to **₹50,000** (see the expense approval limits).

## Hiring
Managers raise a hiring requisition that must be approved by their skip-level and HR before
a role is opened. Offers above the mid-point of the salary band require Leadership sign-off.

## Probation confirmation
Managers must submit a probation confirmation recommendation **two weeks before** the end of
a report's 6-month probation.
