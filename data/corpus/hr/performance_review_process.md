# Performance Review Process

**Owner:** People Team · **Sensitivity:** Confidential · **Audience:** HR, Leadership

## Cycle
Acme runs **two review cycles per year**, concluding in **April** and **October**. Each
cycle covers the preceding six months.

## Rating scale
Performance is rated on a **1 to 5** scale:
- **5 — Outstanding** (top ~10% of employees, calibrated)
- **4 — Exceeds expectations**
- **3 — Meets expectations**
- **2 — Below expectations**
- **1 — Unsatisfactory**

## Calibration
Ratings are normalised across teams in a **9-box** calibration meeting so that managers
apply a consistent bar. No single team may rate more than 15% of its members a "5".

## Promotion and PIP
- Promotion eligibility requires a rating of **4 or higher for two consecutive cycles**.
- An employee rated **2 for two consecutive cycles** is placed on a Performance
  Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 days.

## Documentation
All ratings, feedback and calibration notes are stored confidentially in the HR system and
are accessible only to HR and Leadership.
