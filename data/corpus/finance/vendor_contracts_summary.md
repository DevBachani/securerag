# Vendor Contracts Summary

**Owner:** Finance Team · **Sensitivity:** Confidential · **Audience:** Finance, Leadership

A summary of Acme's largest active vendor contracts for FY27.

## Major contracts
| Vendor | Service | Annual value |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services | Cloud infrastructure | **₹3.2 crore** |
| Microsoft | Productivity & identity | **₹80 lakh** |
| Prestige Estates | Bengaluru HQ office lease | **₹1.1 crore** |
| SecureAudit Pvt Ltd | Annual security audit | **₹25 lakh** |

## Terms
- Standard payment terms are **Net 45** days from invoice.
- Contracts require **60 days' written notice** before renewal or termination.
- Any new contract above ₹50 lakh per year needs CFO approval.

## Risk notes
AWS is the single largest spend and a concentration risk; Finance is evaluating a reserved-
instance commitment to reduce cost by an estimated 18%.
